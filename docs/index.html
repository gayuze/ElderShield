Intelligent Personal Cybersecurity Assistant

ElderShield protects elderly and non-technical smartphone users from deceptive advertisements, fake virus scareware, UPI payment traps, and privacy theft. Built with 100% Kotlin & Jetpack Compose.

View on GitHub Test Live Simulator ↓

Engineered for Explainable Security

No cryptic technical metrics or fake "98% hacked" percentages. Clear, actionable truth in simple Tamil and English.

🛡️

Smart Ad & Scareware OCR

Uses on-device ML Kit vision OCR to analyze screenshots from SMS or WhatsApp. Detects fake virus alerts, lottery prize scams, and extortion language instantly.

🔗

Phishing & URL Inspector

Inspects suspicious links, raw IP-addresses, URL shorteners, and deceptive typosquatting domains (e.g. lookalike banking sites). Never claims HTTPS alone means safe.

📱

Device & Permission Auditor

Audits installed applications in real-time. Highlights sensitive access to Camera, Mic, SMS, and Contacts, and alerts when risky remote-access tools are installed.

💳

UPI & Financial Fraud Guard

Warns against reverse-charge scams (*"Entering UPI PIN always sends money, never receives refunds"*), QR payment tricks, and integrates the 1930 Cybercrime Helpline.

🗣️

Bilingual Voice Guidance

Reads findings aloud via Android TextToSpeech in simple terms. Features full runtime language toggle between English and Tamil (தமிழ்).

📚

Cyber Safety Academy

Interactive bite-sized security lessons and scored quizzes to empower elderly users to identify digital fraud on their own.
